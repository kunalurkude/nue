        Add nue to your home screen

        Android phones and tabletsYour device

        Chrome

        1. Open kunalurkude.github.io/nue in Chrome.
        2. Tap the orange Install app button at the top. If you do not see it, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
        3. Tap Install to confirm.
        4. Open nue from your home screen or app drawer.

        Samsung Internet: tap the ≡ menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

        iPhone and iPadYour device

        Safari

        1. Open kunalurkude.github.io/nue in Safari.
        2. Tap the Share button. On iPhone it is in the bottom bar, on iPad in the top bar.
        3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
        4. Keep Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

        On iOS 16.4 and later, Chrome and Edge can do this too: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

        Windows, Mac and ChromebookYour device

        Chrome or Edge

        1. Open kunalurkude.github.io/nue.
        2. Click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the Install app button at the top of nue.
        3. Click Install.
        4. nue opens in its own window and appears in your Start menu, Launchpad or app list.

        Safari on Mac: File, then Add to Dock.

        Trouble installing?

        I do not see an Install option

        Use the full browser (Chrome or Edge on Android and desktop, Safari on iPhone and iPad). Private windows and the browser built into other apps, such as Instagram or WhatsApp, hide the option. Open the link in your normal browser instead.

        How do I remove it?

        Press and hold the nue icon, then choose Remove or Uninstall. On desktop, open nue and use the menu in its window.

        Do I get updates?

        Yes. When a new version is ready, nue shows an Update button at the bottom of the screen.

        Where is my data stored?

        Your topics, saved stories and watchlist stay on your device. Nothing is sent to an account.
